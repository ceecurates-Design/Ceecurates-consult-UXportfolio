Case Study 1

Rebuilding Lives Foster Family Agency

UX design for a Southern California non-profit dedicated to retaining resource parents and empowering foster youth through community and knowledge.

  • UX Research
  • Full Redesign
  • Non-Profit
  • Foster Care

The Problem

As a non-profit organization, the agency needs to retain resource parents, create an interactive experience rather than a job, and provide ongoing support to resource (foster) families navigating the ever-changing foster care system.

The agency earns more funding from the county when more resource homes are successful, and more youth are positively reunited with their biological families.

The Solution

Help users understand they are not alone. Post-training facts about Foster Care SOP's via Southern California, San Bernardino County law. Motivate singles, LGBTQ, and small families to adopt youth in need.

Tackle the negative stigma of foster care by incorporating the “Rebuilding” mission of RBL FFA. The company wants youth to become thriving, contributing members of society, both in placement and once they age out.

After growing out of foster care, Shelly has 12 extra months to receive benefits that help her into adulthood, but she doesn't know where to start and get ahead.

05 / Research

Research / Pain Points

Interview

“Christina and James are second-round resource parents who must house a youth that has been in the foster care system half their life. The child will age out soon at 18. They are dedicated but having trouble on how to assist this first-time scenario by being compliant and getting the youth ready for adulthood long term.”

Pain points

  1. High risk of burnout due to stress of achieving or failing a youth.
  2. Training only happens once but each youth is a case-by-case scenario.
  3. Youth who want to educate themselves cannot or do not have resources.
  4. The complexity of child welfare, legal, and educational systems often leaves caregivers overwhelmed.
2,767Foster children in California reported missing (runaway) at end of 2020
~5%Of the total foster population in Southern CA, with high rates of repeat incidents
29%Of LA transition-age homeless youth come from the foster system

06 / Strategy

Solution Strategy

“Foster care is not just any job. It's a life-long career.”

My approach to this design was to emphasize the message: you are not alone. The agency is your network, and knowledge is the goal.

My constraints were set to Southern CA Foster Care Law and Bills, to not over-complicate the UI, as it would be used by kids and non-tech-savvy adults using the app.

Design principles

  • Useful
  • Equitable

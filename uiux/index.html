Interactive Barber Landing

“G” Cutz Barber

This one is my most recent project. I pushed the envelope while keeping it as simple as possible for users who just want to book haircuts with as little friction as possible. This landing page has three tabs, with the homepage holding CTA buttons that link to all tabs without the user scrolling back up or down. The CTA shows the most popular cut clients request from G, and an auto-scrolling image carousel of his work directly on the homepage so potential clients can see what they are paying for. G requested that his booking page link to his Square domain, as it currently integrates more easily with his booking calendar. Simple, clean, and sharp personality. Made Specifically with Claude and my Design Thinking.

Rebuilding UI

Rebuilding Lives FFA

I reimagined Rebuilding Lives' digital presence using human centered design thinking mapping the journeys of prospective foster parents, youth seeking resources, and community donors to uncover where the old site lost them. The result is a bold, welcoming experience anchored in the agency's brand colors: nurturing purple and energizing lime green. Vivid visual hierarchy, clear pathways to action, and an emotionally resonant storytelling structure transformed casual visitors into committed members. Since launch, the agency has seen a measurable lift in foster parent inquiries and donation conversions, proving that purposeful design is one of the most powerful tools a nonprofit has.

Fresh Start,

A New Beginning FFA

As the lead UX designer for the redesign of A New Beginning Foster Therapeutic Agency, a 20-year-old organization, I conducted a comprehensive UX audit to identify usability, accessibility, content hierarchy, and conversion issues across an outdated website experience. Through stakeholder interviews, competitive analysis, user journey mapping, information architecture restructuring, and responsive design improvements, I transformed the site into a modern, mission-driven digital experience that better supports foster families, volunteers, donors, and community partners. The redesign improved navigation clarity, content discoverability, mobile usability, and engagement with key agency services while preserving the organization's mission of helping rebuild the lives of children in foster care.
